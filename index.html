ABOUT TRANSFAB

Built in Ajman.
Expanded in Dubai.

Transfab Transport LLC is a UAE-based road transportation company serving businesses, factories, free zones, ports and industrial customers.

Established in 2023 in Ajman Free Zone, we have expanded to a Dubai Mainland LLC, strengthening our ability to support local UAE movements and GCC cross-border transportation.
